Cherry Blossom

h1 {color: white; text-align: center;}
The cherry blossom, or sakura, is the flower of trees in Prunus subgenus Cerasus. Sakura usually refers to flowers of ornamental cherry trees, such as cultivars of Prunus serrulata, not trees grown for their fruit. Many cherry species and cultivars bloom between March and April in the Northern Hemisphere. Sakura trees are often called Japanese cherry in English. Alongside centuries-old traditions that celebrate this alluring flower, the cherry blossom season typically runs from late March to the middle of April, although some seasons may continue into May.
